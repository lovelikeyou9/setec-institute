ABOUT COOLTECH

From small beginnings in downtown Michigan to a comprehensive technology service provider, we've been helping clients achieve their technical goals for over a decade.

Our Story

CoolTECH started as a small technology firm in downtown Michigan, aiming to help businesses make the most of their new digital infrastructure. It soon became obvious that it would make sense to help our clients see beyond the hardware and software, and be there with them from the ground up.

Currently, we offer programming, mechatronics, and robotics engineering services in order to help our customers implement innovative and automated solutions as seamlessly and painlessly as possible.

We value our customers above everything else, meaning that we won't take 'OK' as an answer.
