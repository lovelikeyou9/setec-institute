Disadvantage

DSDM (Dynamic Systems Development Method) is an Agile project management 
and software development framework that focuses on fast delivery, user 
involvement, and iterative development.


01
Requires Active User

- Users must be available frequently.
- If users are not involved, the project may fail.

02
Not Suitable Small

- DSDM has many roles and processes.
- It may be too complex for small or simple projects.

03
Requires Experience

- Continuous testing and reviews are done during development.
- Problems can be detected and fixed early.

04
Higher Cost

- Continuous meetings, testing, and user involvement can increase project cost.

05
Difficult for Fixed Budget Projects

- Because requirements can change, it may be hard to maintain strict cost control.

06
Documentation May Be Limited

- Agile focus on working software may reduce detailed documentation.

07
Not Ideal for Large Distributed Teams

- DSDM requires frequent communication and collaboration.
- This can be difficult when teams are in different locations.
