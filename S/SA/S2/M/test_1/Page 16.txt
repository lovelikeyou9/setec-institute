DSDM & Waterfall

Important differences between them


01
Iterative Development

- Development is done in repeated cycles and improvements.

02
Flexible Requirements

- Changes can be made during development.

03
High User Involvement

- Users participate and give feedback regularly.

04
Early Delivery

- Working software is delivered in small parts quickly.



01
Sequential Development

- Each phase is completed step by step.

02
Fixed Requirements

- Changes are difficult after the project begins.

03
Low User Involvement

- Users are mostly involved at the beginning.

04
Late Delivery

- The full system is delivered at the end of the project.
