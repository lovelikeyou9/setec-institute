05
Build Incrementally

Before major development, teams should understand the business problem and 
proposed solution clearly, but avoid excessive detail that delays progress.

06
Develop Iteratively

Iterative delivery is central to DSDM, recognizing that solutions improve over time, so embracing 
change helps teams refine and deliver more accurate results.

07
Communicate Continuously

Strong communication drives project success—use daily stand-ups, workshops, and early 
models or prototypes to share progress, and encourage regular face-to-face interaction to 
avoid misunderstandings.

08
Demonstrate Control

Show control of the project by making plans and progress visible, using formal tracking 
and reporting, and measuring success by delivered products rather than completed tasks.
