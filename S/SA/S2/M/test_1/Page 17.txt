When to Use DSDM?

DSDM is an Agile project management and software development method.


01
When Requirements May Change

Use DSDM when project requirements are not fully clear at the 
beginning or may change during development.

02
When Fast Delivery is Needed

DSDM is suitable when the project must be delivered quickly.

- Work is divided into short development cycles (iterations).
- Important features are delivered first.

03
When Users Can Participate Actively

Use DSDM when users or clients can be involved in the project.

- Users give continuous feedback.
- Developers improve the system based on feedback.

04
When the Project Needs Clear Priorities

DSDM uses MoSCoW prioritization:
- Must Have
- Should Have
- Could Have
- Won’t Have (for now)

Ex: This helps teams focus on important features first.
