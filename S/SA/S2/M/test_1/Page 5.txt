Core Philosophy

DSDM has 8 core principles


01
Focus on Business Need

Always align decisions with project goals; use Timeboxing and MoSCoW prioritization to 
deliver the right business needs at the right time.

02
Deliver on Time

Delivering products on time is essential for projects; plan timeboxes in advance and set
fixed delivery dates while adjusting features based on business priorities.

03
Cooperate

Teams should work collaboratively, involve subject matter experts, and use facilitated 
workshops to share knowledge with stakeholders & support better decision-making.

04
Never Compromise Quality

In DSDM, quality standards are agreed at the start, and work should be “good enough” by 
building quality through early, continuous testing and regular reviews.
