Business Roles

These roles represent the business side (users and stakeholders). 
They ensure the system delivers real business value.


01
Business Sponsor

- The most senior person responsible for the project.
- Provides funding and resources.
- Makes final business decisions.
- Ensures the project supports business goals.


02
Business Visionary

- Defines the overall vision of the system.
- Ensures the project solves the real business problem.
- Guides the team about what the system should achieve.


03
Business Ambassador

- Represents the daily needs of users.
- Works closely with the development team.

Think of this role like a bridge between users and developers.


04
Business Advisor

- Provides specialized knowledge.
- Helps the team understand specific business processes.

Example:
- Accountant
- Sales expert
