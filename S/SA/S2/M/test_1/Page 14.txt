Advantage

DSDM (Dynamic Systems Development Method) is an Agile project management 
and software development framework that focuses on fast delivery, user 
involvement, and iterative development.


01
Fast Delivery

- DSDM uses timeboxing, meaning work must be completed within a fixed time period.
- This helps deliver software quickly and on schedule.


02
Strong User Involve

- Users are involved throughout the project.
- Their feedback helps ensure the system meets real business needs.

03
Improved Quality

- Continuous testing and reviews are done during development.
- Problems can be detected and fixed early.

04
Flexible to Changes

- Requirements can change during development.
- DSDM handles changes using the MoSCoW prioritization technique 
(Must have, Should have, Could have, Won’t have).

05
Clear Project Control

- The method defines roles and responsibilities clearly (Project Manager, 
Developer, Tester, Business User, etc.).
- This helps manage the project effectively.

06
Reduced Risk

- Frequent deliveries and reviews help identify risks early.
- Issues can be solved before they become big problems.

07
Better Communication

- Team members, stakeholders, and users communicate regularly.
- This improves understanding and collaboration.
