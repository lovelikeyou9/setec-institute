What is DSDM
DSDM was developed in 1994 by the DSDM Consortium in the United Kingdom.

01
What is

Dynamic Systems Development Method (DSDM) is an Agile project 
management and software development framework that focuses on 
delivering business value quickly and efficiently

02
Simple Definition

DSDM is an Agile method that delivers working solutions in small 
stages while keeping strong focus on business needs and deadlines

03
Main Purpose

- Deliver the most important features first
- Keep projects on schedule
- Involve users continuously
- Maintain high quality

04
Key Characteristics

- Iterative & incremental development
- Timeboxing (fixed time periods)
- MoSCoW prioritization (Must, Should, Could, Won’t)
- Strong collaboration between team and users
