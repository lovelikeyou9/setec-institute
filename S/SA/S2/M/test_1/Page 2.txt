📜 History of DSDM

The Dynamic Systems Development Method (DSDM) was created 
in 1994 in the United Kingdom as a response to problems with 
traditional software development methods that were often slow, 
expensive, and inflexible.

⭐ Influence on Modern Agile

For manage the process of developing a system for a business.

What DSDM is used for
DSDM is used to manage and organize software development 
   projects, for example:
	- ✅ Develop business software systems
	- ✅ Manage project teams and tasks
	- ✅ Plan, build, test, and deliver software

What DSDM is used for
   - ❌ It does not create a business
   - ❌ It does not write code itself
   - ❌ It does not manage employees in a company
