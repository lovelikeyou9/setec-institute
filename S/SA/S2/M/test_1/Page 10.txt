MoSCoW

DSDM uses MoSCoW method to prioritize requirements


01
Must Have

Must Have requirements are critical and essential for the system to work.

Characteristics:
- Absolutely necessary
- System cannot run without it
- Required for legal, safety, core
- Highest priority


02
Should Have

Should Have requirements are important but not critical.
The system can still work without them.

Characteristics:
- High priority but not essential
- Can be delayed temporarily
- System still works without them


03
Could Have

Could Have requirements are desirable but optional. They are
usually added if there is extra time or resources.

Characteristics:
- Nice to have
- Low priority
- Easy to remove if needed


04
Won’t Have

Won’t Have requirements are features that will not be
implemented in the current release.

Characteristics:
- Not included in this version
- May be implemented later
- Helps prevent scope creep
